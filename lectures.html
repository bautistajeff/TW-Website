Topic 01

Technical Writing: Definition and Purpose

Technical writing communicates specialized information clearly and accurately so a particular audience can understand a subject or complete a task.

Inform

Present facts, findings, or instructions the reader needs.

Guide

Help readers complete a process safely and correctly.

Support decisions

Organize evidence so readers can compare options and act.

Example

A setup guide explains a device in the order a new user needs: identify the parts, connect them, and check that the device works.
