About the author

I enjoy turning complex ideas into clear, useful information.

I am Jeffrey A. Bautista, a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems student at Institute of Library and Information System. I am interested in technical writing because I enjoy learning how to explain difficult topics in a way that is clear, structured, and helpful to others.

Through this website, I aim to share my knowledge and understanding of technical writing with others. I believe that when information is organized well and written with purpose, it becomes easier for people to learn and apply it.

Learning principle

Be clear. Be accurate. Keep the reader in mind.
